# KEMO Steel Industries — Business Website

A client website developed for **KEMO Steel Industries**, a steel manufacturer based in Kutch, Gujarat, India.

🌐 **Live website:** https://kemosteels.com/

## About the Client

KEMO Steel Industries manufactures and supplies steel products for construction, infrastructure and industrial applications. The company's website highlights its steel billets and TMT bars, including TMT 550D products.

Its business positioning highlights:

- Manufacturing of MS steel billets
- Production of TMT reinforcement bars
- German Thermex® technology for TMT bar processing
- Quality testing and product certification information
- Domestic supply and international export enquiries

The website describes the company's manufacturing capabilities, quality processes, products and export services.

## Website Overview

The website serves as a digital company profile and product information platform. It helps prospective customers, builders, infrastructure companies, dealers and international buyers learn about the business and make enquiries.

### Main Website Sections

- **Home:** Company introduction, key business highlights and product overview.
- **About:** Company background and manufacturing experience.
- **Products:** Steel billets and TMT reinforcement bars.
- **Manufacturing:** Overview of the production process and Thermex® technology.
- **Quality & Certifications:** Quality assurance and certification information.
- **Export:** International supply capabilities and export information.
- **Gallery:** Visual presentation of products and manufacturing facilities.
- **Contact:** Business contact details and enquiry information.
